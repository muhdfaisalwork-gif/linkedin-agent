Method 1: Instant Cookie Connect (< 1 sec) Recommended

Since you are already logged into LinkedIn in your browser (Opera, Chrome, or Edge), simply paste your li_at session cookie. No windows, zero wait time!

📖 How to copy your li_at cookie in 10 seconds
  1. Open your LinkedIn tab in Opera / Chrome / Edge where you are already signed in.
  2. Press F12 (or Right Click → Inspect).
  3. Click the Application tab (in Firefox/Edge it may be called Storage).
  4. In the left sidebar, expand Cookies → click https://www.linkedin.com.
  5. Find the row named li_at, double click its Value, copy it, and paste it above!
